# Operating costs and service decisions

Planning assumptions checked November 26, 2026. Prices below are published examples in USD unless marked PKR. No paid account is necessary for local development. Current Azure subscription charges, Atlas account tier, contracts, taxes and any domain renewal are unknown. Never treat an existing account or a free trial as spending authority.

| Capability | Development and pilot choice | Limit, cost trigger and fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App and identity | Existing Next.js standalone process and Better Auth in the app. Local processes and MongoDB cover both email/password and session tests. Retain the Azure artifact contract. | Azure Linux F1 lists $0, 60 CPU minutes/day and no SLA. Microsoft excludes production workloads. Quote a supported regional tier and backup plan before real bookings. Self-hosting would transfer OS patching, TLS, uptime and restores to the owner. [Azure Linux pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/app-service/linux/) |
| Database | Local MongoDB replica set covers transactions without a subscription. Existing Atlas is a candidate once TLS and a restore drill pass. No new search engine, cache or hosted message queue. | Atlas Free has 0.5 GB including indexes and restricts operations. Free backups are private preview and should not be assumed available. Measure data plus indexes, verify export and isolated restore, then quote tier, region, storage and backups before real funds. [Atlas limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/) |
| Video | Keep provider-independent join authorization, room UI, reports and reconnect handling testable locally. Agora is the candidate for a two-person browser session. No cloud recording, forced proxy or analytics add-on. | New RTC Free includes 10,000 standard minutes with service suspension on excess. HD costs four standard minutes per participant minute. A 30-minute two-person HD lesson uses about 240, so 40 lessons use 9,600 and 100 use 24,000. At that quality the free package stops during lesson 42 under these assumptions. Published Starter is $45.99 monthly for 50,000 standard minutes, with overage terms. Screen sharing, quality changes, legacy contracts and other apps on the account change this estimate. Before a real pilot verify plan, hard stop, terms and how a lesson can finish safely. A self-hosted WebRTC relay still needs bandwidth, monitoring and on-call support. [RTC billing](https://docs.agora.io/en/realtime-media/rtc/reference/pricing) · [Packages and suspension](https://www.agora.io/en/pricing/video-calling/) |
| Mail | Local capture for isolated tests. Prefer a small transactional API adapter and a verified sending domain for production. No marketing list, domain purchase or delivery account needed for local QA. | Resend lists 3,000 emails/month and 100/day free. Four emails per lesson at 100 lessons/month would be 400 before signups and retries. Daily bursts can still breach the cap. Pro lists $20/month for 50,000. Postmark's developer plan offers 100/month. Check commercial terms, domain ownership, deliverability and hard limits before enabling either. Never rely on production verification links in logs. [Resend pricing](https://resend.com/pricing) · [Postmark pricing](https://postmarkapp.com/pricing/) |
| Payments and payouts | Continue with the signed local simulator. Provider contract, marketplace settlement and KYC are external gates. Store each currency separately. Payout approval and settlement are distinct. | Safepay Standard lists 2.9% + PKR 30 domestic and an additional 0.3% international. At a PKR 3,000 international charge that is about PKR 126 before taxes, FX, disputes and payout costs. Ten such charges would be PKR 1,260, 100 would be PKR 12,600. Negotiated terms may differ. No setup/monthly fee on published Standard. Actual merchant eligibility and effective fixed fees matter especially for a cheap intro. [Safepay prices](https://safepay.com.pk/pricing) · [Merchant evaluation](payment-providers.md) |
| Messaging, learning and observability | Participant-only MongoDB text, bounded reads and refresh while the booking is open. Keep progress notes as small text fields and optional links. Existing host logs with redaction, bounded retention and manual export are sufficient for development. | No separate SaaS fee for these functions. Database storage, queries, host resources and operational attention still grow with usage. Bound message length, polling, retention and fan-out before pilot. No recording, uploads, paid LLM, public code execution, paid monitoring or third-party analytics needed for the pilot proposition. |

At zero lessons, run locally with zero additional provider commitments. At ten 30-minute HD lessons, two attendees use about 2,400 standard minutes. At 100 lessons the free video cap binds even though modelled email traffic fits the free monthly allocation. These are capacity estimates, not a production budget. A suitable hosting plan, restore capability and merchant acceptance still need independent verification. Attach a scale decision to observed traffic, peak day and support effort.

**Owner approval is mandatory before paid-dependent implementation or activation.** Present the need, free alternative, expected fixed and variable costs, commercial terms and risks. Wait for affirmative approval for any card, billing activation, paid plan, recurring subscription or spend. Continue independent local work. Track vendor-specific code at the existing mail, payment and future RTC boundaries. Recheck current terms, commercial-use eligibility, quota enforcement and cancellation before entering an agreement.
