# SkillNest product discovery

Research checkpoint, November 26, 2026. The subsequent owner consultation informed the [pilot specification](pilot-spec.md). The proposals below are hypotheses for the pilot. The existing [decisions](decisions.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md), and [execution record](execution.md) describe the project before that consultation.

## What the student and tutor can do today

| Area | Evidence and product consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | A verified student can filter, save and compare approved tutors. Cards show an hourly rate and lesson lengths. A student must open a tutor and the booking page to find a real opening and the price for that duration. With the currency filter empty, price sorting compares raw numbers across currencies. A budget-conscious learner cannot reliably identify the cheapest comparable lesson that way. See [catalog](../app/tutors/page.tsx), [query](../lib/tutors.ts), and [booking form](../components/tutors/lesson-hold-form.tsx). |
| Tutor supply and trust | Tutors save a draft, set hours and submit a profile for staff review. Changes to weekly hours can take an approved tutor out of discovery until review. Private booking settings can be updated independently. This creates a real staffing cost even when the tutor only wants to change their timetable. See [tutor actions](../actions/tutors.ts) and [moderation decisions](decisions.md). |
| Booking and money | The committed baseline at `a8e6bc3` includes timezone-safe holds, overlap protection for both participants, signed local sandbox events, and separate commission and pending earnings entries. The preceding session verified these with isolated MongoDB and an Edge student journey. Cancellation, refund review, and tutor lesson history are additional local, uncommitted work. Their database refund checks passed. The separate refund browser run stopped at an ambiguous login selector before it reached cancellation. |
| Delivery and operations | Booking-linked messaging, the Agora classroom, completed-lesson reviews, real checkout and payouts are outstanding. Atlas TLS, hosted Google OAuth, production email, merchant acceptance, and final commercial and safeguarding policies remain open in the execution record. “Confirmed booking,” “lesson delivered,” and “tutor may withdraw earnings” are three different events that the future UI needs to explain. |
| Interface | A fresh read-only Edge inspection of the existing homepage build at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 showed a warm editorial design and a prominent student action at both sizes. “Find Your Tutor” opens signup. Five decorative stars and “verified tutors” appear before the student sees what staff actually reviewed. The public How It Works page also describes classroom access as available. The first improvement is to tell prospective students what a real first lesson can accomplish and how tutor approval works. See [homepage](../app/page.tsx) and [How It Works](../app/how-it-works/page.tsx). |

The current Next.js application already keeps Better Auth identity data separate from marketplace records. MongoDB transactions protect reservations and sandbox accounting. Nothing uncovered during discovery requires a platform rewrite. The immediate design questions concern what happens around those transactions.

## What other marketplaces teach us

| Product | Published behavior | Implication to test at SkillNest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preply | Learners can filter by specialty and compatible times. Tutors apply with a profile and teaching information. A first trial is charged separately, subsequent subscriptions renew every 28 days, and the platform takes the full trial fee as commission. [Discovery](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4179351-find-the-right-tutor-for-you) · [Tutor application](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4175138-register-to-teach-on-preply) · [Billing](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/15432866-how-and-when-will-i-be-charged) · [Commission](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171383-preply-commission-model) | Let students see a specific teaching specialty, a compatible local time, and the lesson total together. Give tutors a preview of what they would earn on a first lesson. An introductory offer needs an explicit decision about who pays for the tutor's time. |
| italki | Its developer listing describes payment per class, teacher-set prices and search by interest and specialization. Learners can choose conversation, exam or professional goals within the same language. [Developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.italki.app&hl=en) | Single-lesson purchasing already exists elsewhere. Test whether Pakistani tutor supply gives SkillNest a particular strength, such as teaching a particular exam or explaining a topic bilingually. A generic catalog cannot demonstrate that advantage. |
| Wyzant | Its first-lesson guarantee can refund the first hour with a new tutor. Its studio supports exporting the whiteboard, text or code worked on during a lesson. [First-lesson guarantee](https://support.wyzant.com/students-parents/lessons/whats-the-good-fit-guarantee/) · [Lesson work](https://support.wyzant.com/online-learning-studio/whiteboard-faqs/can-i-save-work-from-the-whiteboard/) | A clear remedy can reduce first-booking uncertainty, but someone must review and fund it. The useful classroom tool depends on the learner's task. An exam candidate might want their worked solution, while a speaking student might want three corrections to practice. |
| Superprof US | Students request a tutor through a contact subscription, charged when a tutor accepts the request. The charge covers access to tutors. [Student Pass](https://www.superprof.com/help/students/student-pass-subscription/subscribe-to-the-student-pass-to-contact-a-tutor/36/) | Explicitly distinguish sending a question, holding a time and paying for teaching. A student should be able to name what each button commits them to before pressing it. |
| TeacherOn | Tutors can receive inbound inquiries free. Some outbound leads cost coins. The platform explains that charging for some contacts limits the flood of responses students receive. [Teacher guide](https://www.teacheron.com/guide/how-it-works-teachers) | If pre-booking contact matters, test a student-initiated question to one selected tutor. This helps a learner establish fit and limits speculative outreach and unpaid work for tutors. |
| Outschool | Parents have a channel for logistical questions and private tutoring discussions about learning plans. Its safety guidance describes learner identity and parent participation rules. [Family safety guidance](https://support.outschool.com/en/articles/579976-learner-safety-and-privacy-for-parents) | Serving families changes who buys, attends, messages a tutor, and receives follow-up. Decide the intended age group before specifying messaging permissions or classroom access. |

**Reported friction.** Separate italki reviewers describe classroom lag or uncertainty over whether the other person has joined. Preply documents how to investigate unexpected charges and how a student can report a failed lesson before payment is released to the tutor. These are examples of failure modes. Their frequency among SkillNest's prospective users is unknown. Useful pilot checks are whether a learner can recover from a lost connection, find help on the booking, and understand the payment outcome. [italki firsthand reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/italki-language-learning/id1140000003?see-all=reviews) · [Preply billing help](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/15432110-why-was-i-charged-when-i-didn-t-expect-it) · [Preply lesson confirmation](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4179402-how-to-confirm-a-lesson)

## What to learn from the owner and pilot

- **First market.** Identify one student need the first tutors can serve well and a realistic way to reach both sides. The source material establishes international students and Pakistan-based tutors. It does not establish which subject, country or price range has demand.
- **First booking.** Learn what evidence makes it reasonable to pay a new tutor. A strong example profile, a brief question, and a shorter paid first lesson solve different problems.
- **Learning and repeat use.** Ask what a student should take away from an actual lesson. Test a tutor-written next step and an easy return booking before investing in a full learning platform.
- **Operations.** Walk through one missed lesson from both sides. Measure how often staff must intervene, what evidence is available, and whether each person understands the outcome. For tutors, distinguish a collected student payment, pending earnings and a completed payout. [Example tutor earnings workflow](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171348-when-and-how-you-get-paid-for-lessons)
- **Pilot evidence.** Observe learners finding a suitable tutor and local time, stating the total correctly, attending a first lesson and deciding whether to rebook. Ask tutors whether their first bookings justified the preparation and administrative time. Set targets after the owner identifies the audience and the pilot establishes a baseline.

The owner selected adult university learners, a Python-first candidate and a preference for public exploration and demonstrable progress. The [pilot specification](pilot-spec.md) records the resulting decisions.
